Apple Inc. is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. 
Apple was founded by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne on April 1, 1976, to develop and sell Wozniak's Apple I personal computer.
The company was incorporated by Jobs and Wozniak as Apple Computer, Inc. in January 1977.
Steve Jobs served as CEO from 1997 until 2011. Tim Cook became CEO in 2011.

Microsoft Corporation is an American multinational technology corporation headquartered in Redmond, Washington.
Microsoft was founded by Bill Gates and Paul Allen on April 4, 1975, to develop and sell BASIC interpreters for the Altair 8800.
Bill Gates served as CEO from 1975 until 2000. Steve Ballmer was CEO from 2000 to 2014. Satya Nadella became CEO in 2014.

Google LLC is an American multinational technology company focusing on artificial intelligence, online advertising, search engine technology, cloud computing, and more.
Google was founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin while they were Ph.D. students at Stanford University in California.
The company was officially founded on September 4, 1998. Sundar Pichai became CEO in 2015.

Facebook (now Meta Platforms) is an American multinational technology conglomerate based in Menlo Park, California.
Facebook was founded by Mark Zuckerberg along with his Harvard College roommates in 2004.
Mark Zuckerberg has served as CEO since the company's inception.
